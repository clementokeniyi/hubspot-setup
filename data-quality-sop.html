Data Quality SOP: CRM Hygiene Standards

Fyzer Co HubSpot Account · Prepared by C Okeniyi, RevOps Admin · October 2026
Overview
Purpose
Define the standards and procedures for maintaining clean, accurate, and complete data in the Fyzer Co HubSpot CRM. Poor data quality leads to misdirected outreach, inaccurate forecasting, and broken automations. This SOP covers deduplication, required field standards, naming conventions, import rules, and ongoing audit cadence.

Deduplication rules
Duplicate detection criteria
A duplicate is any record that shares two or more of: same email address, same first + last name + company, or same phone number.
ScenarioAction
Same email, different nameMerge — keep the record with more complete properties. Update name if the newer record is more accurate.
Same name + company, different emailReview manually. If same person, merge and retain both emails. If different person, keep separate.
Company auto-created from contact importMatch to existing company record. Merge if duplicate. Enrich missing fields from the companies import CSV.
Form submission creates new contact who already existsHubSpot auto-merges on matching email. Review merged record within 24h to confirm data accuracy.
Import standards
RuleDetail
Always use the import templateDownload HubSpot's CSV template for each object. Never import a raw spreadsheet without mapping columns to HubSpot properties first.
Validate before importCheck for blank required fields, invalid email formats, and duplicate emails in the CSV before uploading.
Name every importUse format: [Account Name] — [Object] Import — [YYYY-MM]. Example: Fyzer Co — Contacts Import — 2026-10.
Set GDPR legal basis on importAlways select "Legitimate interest — Prospect/Lead" for B2B cold contacts imported from research or events.
Review import errorsDownload the error report immediately after import. Fix and re-import failed rows within 24 hours.
Audit cadence
Daily
Form & pipeline review
  • Check new form submissions in HubSpot Forms → Submissions
  • Verify new contacts created by form are complete and correctly staged
  • Review any deal stage changes for missing close dates or amounts
Weekly
Deduplication & fill rate
  • Run HubSpot's built-in duplicate contacts tool (Contacts → Actions → Manage duplicates)
  • Check Properties fill rates for Email, Job Title, Company — flag any below 80%
  • Review contacts with Lead Status = Open for 7+ days and no activity
  • Confirm all deals have an associated contact and company
Monthly
Full CRM health audit
  • Export all contacts and review for naming convention violations
  • Archive contacts with no activity in 180+ days and Lead Status = Unqualified
  • Review and update RevOps Segment and ICP Score for all MQL contacts
  • Audit pipeline for deals with no activity in 30+ days — mark stale or close
  • Check custom property usage rates in Settings → Properties → Fill Rate column
The Fyzer Co HubSpot account currently has 23 contacts, 20 companies, and 13 deals — all imported with complete required fields and GDPR legal basis set. Property fill rates for Email, First Name, Last Name, Company Name, and Country are at 100% across all imported records. The three custom properties (RevOps Segment, ICP Score, Outreach Cadence) are available for manual scoring and segmentation.
