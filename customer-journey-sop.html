Customer Journey Map: B2B SaaS Buyer

Fyzer Co HubSpot Account · Prepared by C Okeniyi, RevOps Admin · October 2026
Persona
Primary persona
The RevOps Manager
Mid-market B2B company, 50–500 employees. Owns the CRM, sales process, and pipeline reporting. Frustrated by data silos and manual reporting.
Goals
Clean pipeline data · Accurate forecasts · Automated handoffs · Fewer manual updates · Prove marketing ROI
Pain points
Messy CRM data · Reps not updating deals · Marketing ≠ Sales alignment · No visibility into attribution · Too many tools
