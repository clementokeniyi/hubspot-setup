Lead Scoring SOP: HubSpot Score Model

Fyzer Co HubSpot Account · Prepared by C Okeniyi, RevOps Admin · October 2026
Overview
Purpose
Automatically score every contact based on their demographic fit and behavioural engagement so the sales team can prioritise outreach on the hottest leads. The HubSpot Score property updates in real time as contacts take actions or their properties change. Contacts scoring 50+ are flagged for immediate sales follow-up.
Scoring model type
Manual / rule-based HubSpot Score using positive and negative attribute scoring. Future state: replace with Breeze AI Predictive Lead Scoring (available on Enterprise) once the dataset reaches 200+ MQL contacts.

This SOP documents the full lead scoring model for Fyzer Co. On the current free plan, scores are tracked manually using the custom ICP Score property created in this account (Settings → Properties → ICP Score). The scoring criteria above serve as the manual scoring rubric reps use when updating that field. Full automation activates on upgrade to Professional via the HubSpot Score property and workflow triggers.
