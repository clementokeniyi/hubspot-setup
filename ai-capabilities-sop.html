HubSpot AI (Breeze) Capabilities Documentation

Fyzer Co HubSpot Account · Prepared by C Okeniyi, RevOps Admin · October 2026
Overview
HubSpot's AI suite is branded Breeze, consisting of Breeze Copilot, Breeze Agents, and Breeze Intelligence. These tools automate time-consuming RevOps tasks including content creation, contact data enrichment, deal forecasting, and predictive lead scoring. This document maps each Breeze feature to its plan requirement, use case, and how Fyzer Co would leverage it at scale.
Breeze feature map by plan
✍️ AI Content Assistant
Free
AI writing assistant built into email templates, forms, landing pages, and blogs. Rewrites, expands, or shortens selected text. Generates subject line suggestions.
Fyzer Co use case: Draft cold outreach email templates, generate demo request follow-up email subject lines, write company descriptions on import.
💬 ChatSpot (Breeze Copilot)
Free
Conversational AI assistant inside HubSpot. Use plain-language commands to create contacts, pull reports, summarise deals, draft emails, and search the CRM. Accessible via the chat icon in the HubSpot top bar.
Fyzer Co use case: "Show me all contacts in the SaaS industry with lifecycle stage = MQL", "Create a summary of the Nexara Technologies deal", "Draft a follow-up email for James Okafor".
🎯 Predictive Lead Scoring
Enterprise
Breeze automatically scores contacts based on historical closed-won deal patterns. Replaces manual rule-based HubSpot Score. Updates in real time as contact behaviour changes. Requires 200+ closed deals for the model to train on.
Fyzer Co use case: Auto-prioritise the top 10% of leads for same-day outreach. Surface rising contacts before SDRs manually check. Replace manual ICP Score property on upgrade to Enterprise.
📊 AI Forecast Assist
Professional
Breeze analyses deal activity, stage velocity, and historical close rates to generate an AI-assisted revenue forecast. Shows a predicted commit range alongside the manual forecast in the Deals view.
Fyzer Co use case: Provide CRO with a weekly AI-assisted pipeline call that flags deals at risk of slipping based on no recent activity or stalled stage progression.
🤖 Breeze Prospecting Agent
Professional
AI agent that researches a company or contact, pulls public information, and drafts a personalised outreach email. Works from inside a contact record. Uses publicly available web data and LinkedIn signals.
Fyzer Co use case: Before an SDR makes an outreach call, run Prospecting Agent on the company to auto-populate deal context, pain points, and a personalised first email.
🧠 Breeze Intelligence (Data Enrichment)
Add-on
Automatically enriches contact and company records with missing data — job titles, company size, industry, technologies used — sourced from Breeze's proprietary data network. Reduces manual data entry. Charged per enriched record.
Fyzer Co use case: After a form submission creates a bare-bones contact (just email + name), Breeze Intelligence auto-fills company, job title, employee count — enabling instant lead scoring without SDR research time.
📧 Breeze Email Agent
Enterprise
Fully autonomous AI agent that monitors the inbox, classifies replies, auto-drafts responses for review, and handles routine email tasks (schedule meeting, send collateral, answer FAQs). Human review required before sending.
Fyzer Co use case: Route inbound replies from sequences — classify as "interested", "not interested", "out of office", "referral" — and auto-draft the appropriate next step for the SDR to approve.
🔍 AI Deal Summary
Free
One-click AI summary of any deal record, synthesising all associated emails, calls, notes, and meetings into a concise status update. Accessible from the Deal record sidebar.
Fyzer Co use case: Before any deal review meeting, reps pull a 30-second AI summary instead of scrolling through 3 months of activity. Used for handoffs from SDR to AE, and AE to CS on close.
All four "Free" tier AI features (Content Assistant, Breeze Copilot, AI Deal Summary, and basic email generation) are available in the current Fyzer Co free demo account. The Professional and Enterprise features are documented here as the target-state architecture for a scaled RevOps team.
