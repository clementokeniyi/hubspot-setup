Sequences SOP: Cold Outreach — Demo Request

Fyzer Co HubSpot Account · Prepared by C Okeniyi, RevOps Admin · October 2026
Overview
Purpose
Enrol every qualified prospect into a 5-step, 14-day outreach sequence after a demo request or manual sales enrolment. The sequence alternates between personalised emails and manual call tasks to maximise touch points without over-contacting. A contact is automatically unenrolled the moment they reply or book a meeting.
Enrolment trigger
Sales rep manually enrols a contact from the contact record or from the Sequences dashboard. Contacts with Lifecycle Stage = MQL and Lead Status = Open are eligible.
Sequence map — 5 steps over 14 days
⚡
Enrolment
Contact enrolled by sales rep
Rep enrols the contact from their HubSpot record or the Sequences tab. Enrolment uses the rep's connected inbox so all emails are sent from their address.
1
Step 1 — Day 1 · Email
Cold intro: "Quick question about {{company}}'s revenue ops"
Personalised cold email referencing company name and pain point. Template: Cold Outreach — Demo Request (saved in Message Templates). Sent immediately on enrolment.
⏱
Delay
Wait 2 business days
If no reply, move to Step 2.
2
Step 2 — Day 3 · Call task
Manual task: call the prospect
Creates a task for the rep to make a call. Call script: reference the email sent on Day 1, ask about current RevOps stack, offer to schedule a 20-min demo. Log outcome as Connected or No Answer.
⏱
Delay
Wait 3 business days
If no connection, move to Step 3.
3
Step 3 — Day 6 · Email
Value email: "How teams like yours cut deal cycle time by 30%"
Shares a one-paragraph case study or stat relevant to the prospect's industry. Soft CTA: "Would a quick overview be useful?" No hard sell.
⏱
Delay
Wait 3 business days
If no reply, move to Step 4.
4
Step 4 — Day 9 · Email
Social proof email: "What Pipeline IQ said after 60 days with Fyzer"
Short testimonial-style email. One quote from a fictional Fyzer customer. CTA: "Happy to send over a quick overview doc — just say the word."
⏱
Delay
Wait 5 business days
Final wait before breakup email.
5
Step 5 — Day 14 · Email
Breakup email: "Closing the loop"
Short, low-pressure close. "I'll assume the timing isn't right — no worries. I'll leave the door open if things change. You can always book a time here: [Meetings link]." Sets Lead Status → Unqualified on send.
✓
End
Sequence complete — contact unenrolled
Contact exits sequence. If no response across all 5 steps, Lead Status is set to Unqualified. Contact remains in CRM for future re-engagement campaigns.
Auto-unenrolment rules
TriggerAction
Contact replies to any email in the sequenceUnenrol immediately — rep takes over manually
Contact books a meeting via Meetings linkUnenrol immediately — move Lifecycle Stage to Opportunity
Contact unsubscribesUnenrol immediately — set email subscription to unsubscribed
All 5 steps completed with no responseUnenrol — set Lead Status to Unqualified

This SOP was designed for a HubSpot Professional account. In the current free demo environment, the sequence logic is fully documented. Steps 1, 3, 4, and 5 (emails) would be sent manually using the saved Cold Outreach — Demo Request template. Step 2 (call task) is available on the free plan via the Tasks feature.
