# HubSpot RevOps Portfolio — Fyzer Co

**A live, working HubSpot CRM built to show exactly how a RevOps specialist cleans up a sales team's biggest headaches — messy data, stalled pipelines, and zero visibility.**

</div>

---

## The problem this solves

Most B2B sales teams have a CRM nobody trusts. Contacts are incomplete. Deals sit in the wrong stage for weeks. Marketing has no idea if their campaigns are working. Sales reps update nothing because it takes too long.

This portfolio shows how to fix all of that — clean data in, the right automations running in the background, dashboards leadership can actually read, and a sales team that spends time selling instead of doing admin.

13 deals built across a 7-stage pipeline: Prospect → Qualified → Discovery → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won. Every deal has an amount, close date, and is linked to both a contact and a company.

## 06 — HubSpot Form on Live Website

A HubSpot form embedded directly on the Fyzer Co website (hosted on GitHub Pages). Every submission creates a contact record automatically in the CRM — no manual entry, no leads falling through the cracks.

## 09 & 10 — Segmentation

Two live segments built to power targeting and outreach — All Imported Contacts (22 records) and Open Leads. Segments update dynamically as contact properties change.

## 11 — Workflow SOP: Lead Nurture Automation

Documents how a form submission triggers a multi-step lead nurture workflow — with branch logic for Enterprise vs SMB, delays, email sends, and unenrolment conditions.

![Workflow SOP](screenshots/11-workflow-sop.png)

---

## 12 — Sequences SOP: Cold Outreach Cadence

A 5-step, 14-day cold outreach sequence — Day 1 email, Day 3 call task, Day 6 value email, Day 9 social proof, Day 14 breakup email. Includes auto-unenrolment rules on reply or meeting booked.

![Sequences SOP](screenshots/12-sequences-sop.png)

---

## 13 — Lead Scoring SOP

A rule-based lead scoring model with positive attributes (+5 to +30) and negative attributes (−10 to −30). Four tiers: Hot (75+), Warm (50–74), Cool (25–49), Cold (0–24). Includes scored sample contacts from the Fyzer Co CRM.

![Lead Scoring SOP](screenshots/13-lead-scoring-sop.png)

---

## 14 — Data Quality SOP & Property Fill Rates

CRM hygiene standards — required fields by object, naming conventions, deduplication rules, import standards, and a Daily/Weekly/Monthly audit cadence. Property fill rates tracked to maintain data completeness.

![Property Fill Rates](screenshots/14-property-fill-rates.png)

## 15 — Custom Properties

Three custom contact properties built on the free plan: **ICP Score** (numeric, manual lead scoring), **RevOps Segment** (Enterprise / Mid-Market / SMB), and **Outreach Cadence** (High-Touch / Mid-Touch / Low-Touch / Do Not Contact).
