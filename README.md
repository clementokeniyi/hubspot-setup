---

## The problem this solves

Most B2B sales teams have a CRM that nobody trusts. Contacts are incomplete, deals are stuck in the wrong stage, nobody knows which leads to call first, and marketing has no idea if their campaigns are working.

This portfolio shows exactly how a RevOps specialist fixes that — clean data coming in, the right people seeing the right information, automations handling the repetitive work, and dashboards that give leadership a clear view of the pipeline at any point in time.
